# Holiday and expansion guidance

Read this before step 4 of `whatsapp-business-marketing-messages-max-price-setup`. This guide supplies scenario reasoning, not a numeric pricing policy. The currently available account/template tools cannot calculate an evidence-backed holiday uplift or a cohort-specific optimal bid.

## Holiday promotion

Collect the destination countries, actual occasion/date window, selected template, current bid/overrides, business objective and explicit financial limits. Ask for the business's intended dates and timezone; do not invent holiday dates or assume a send window from calendar memory. Ask for aggregate historical delivery/cost/outcome context only if the business has it; do not ask for recipient-level data.

A useful recommendation explains the choice:

- If the business wants to retain its present ceiling or has no evidence supporting a change, explain retaining the current setting as an option. Do not claim it is optimal or guarantees the desired reach.
- If the business explicitly wants to prioritize delivery and is willing to increase its ceiling, explain the trade-off and collect its chosen bid or country multiplier. Holiday context alone does not pick that number.
- If the business wants a lower ceiling, explain that lower willingness to pay may constrain delivery. Ask for its explicit limit; do not predict the effect without supported evidence.

Explain whether a proposed country override affects other traffic using this template. The holiday date window does not schedule the override or restore an old value; the existing sending integration and a separately approved later change own those actions. Never promise a temporary change will end automatically.

## New country

Confirm the new country and whether the business will use the existing template or a separate template. Existing performance in another country is not a validated forecast for the new one. Identify missing local evidence rather than copying a bid blindly.

Explain available base-bid versus country-override choices. A country override does not select recipients, upload a subscriber list or give product eligibility. Collect the business's explicit value and leave eligibility/normalization to the server. Do not automatically raise the base bid for every country to address one new market.

## New audience in the same country

Clarify how the business will distinguish the cohort in its current sending integration. Country multipliers apply to all matching country traffic using the configured template; they cannot target “new customers” while excluding existing customers in that country.

Recommend verifying supported template/integration separation before proposing separate pricing. Do not create a second template just because “new cohort” was mentioned. If the business wants one shared configuration, explain its broader effect and confirm that scope.

## Evidence and limits

Current configuration is not evidence of the best price. A rate card is not an optimal bid, an individual clearing price or an outcome guarantee. A bid cap is not a total spending budget. Aggregate delivery/cost signals are not proof of incremental business value.

If a reviewed server recommendation becomes available, consume its source, freshness, rationale, warnings and normalized arguments without recomputing them in the model. Do not invent a recommendation endpoint, scrape an unsupported source, or paste internal pricing policy into this public skill.

When a numeric recommendation cannot be supported, say so plainly. The user can still choose explicit settings for the existing server-validated template workflow; do not present those user choices as a Meta-calculated recommendation.
